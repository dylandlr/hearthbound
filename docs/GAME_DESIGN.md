# Hearthbound — Game Design v0.2

## Fantasy
A Minecraft-first multiplayer colony simulation inspired by RimWorld and Stardeus. Players are embodied Minecraft characters, building and exploring while named citizens live, learn, work, socialize, and operate real Create machines.

## Design pillars
1. Fun-first, visible gameplay. No subsystem earns its complexity merely by being sophisticated.
2. Citizen autonomy without constant micromanagement.
3. Create is the backbone of industrial progression: expertise, shared procedures, physical capacity.
4. Explore → discover → construct → demonstrate → teach → delegate → improve.
5. Shared multiplayer colony first, rival colonies later.
6. Real server-authoritative inventories, interactions, and outcomes.
7. No fabricated AI capabilities or gameplay footage.

## First playable experience — The First Mill
A riverside settlement with Mara (supplies), Iven (exploration), and Sable (craft/food).
The player constructs a Create mill and registers approved input/output inventories, teaches Sable with a live demonstration, delegates milling, and sees physical flour delivered. A workshop interruption must be recoverable with correct item accounting. An expedition opportunity follows a successful production milestone.

## Advancement
Camp → Hamlet → Village → Workshop Town → Trade Settlement → Industrial City → Civilization.
These are capability descriptions, not artificial recipe gates. Building works according to Minecraft/Create recipes; autonomous operation spreads through certified procedures, mentorship, installed facilities, and accessible supplies.

## Simulation rules
Residents complete mundane jobs through deterministic controllers; personality affects interests and significant decisions but cannot make basic work randomly impossible. Social memory tracks consequential events. Offline policy freezes hazardous progression by default when all members are gone; persistent survival is opt-in.

## Non-goals for initial slice
No unrestricted AI-generated code, arbitrary AI machine design, hundreds of LLM citizens, procedural continental politics, or automatic import of incompatible mods.
