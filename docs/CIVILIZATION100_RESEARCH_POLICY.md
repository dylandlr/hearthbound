# Civilization-100 research policy

Mandatory for every ticket and PR. Preserve the 100-agent embodied emergent civilization mission. Before design, inspect relevant original sources: supplied Drive refs/ (all modules), Project Sid, MARBLE/MultiAgentBench, CSCE 631 and 642 materials, Intelligent Agents and Their Applications, Craftle and prior Hearthbound evidence. Gameplay references include RimWorld, Stardeus and Create. Do not substitute titles or summaries for reading relevant passages.

## Required ticket evidence
- Exact source identity, revision or edition, page/section and accessible link or private locator.
- Supported finding, limitations, and applicability to the current feature.
- Design decision, alternatives, assumptions and explicit distinction between source claims and our hypotheses.
- Testable prediction, baseline, ablation where applicable, and acceptance criteria fixed before held-out evaluation.
- Commit, configuration, seeds, all trial outcomes, physical receipts where applicable, and negative results.
- Review of earlier experiments and regressions; no claims beyond evidence.

If a source is inaccessible, record the blocker; do not invent findings. For non-research tickets, document applicability or justified N/A. Reviewers must reject unsupported claims and missing applicable traceability. This is a review policy, not yet an automated CI gate.

## Lifecycle
Requirements -> design -> test design -> implementation -> review -> QA -> AI validation -> native Minecraft validation -> integration -> acceptance. Mark inapplicable phases with rationale. No closure without evidence. Never weaken acceptance criteria after observing results without a versioned protocol amendment and fresh held-out evaluation.
