# Hearthbound research foundation: Project Sid, MultiAgentBench/MARBLE, and PA4

Reviewed: 22 November 2026 (user client date). This note records source identity, transferable ideas, and the limits of their evidence. It supports [the implementation contract](PA4_MARBLE_IMPLEMENTATION_CONTRACT_V0_2.md). It does not report a new Hearthbound experiment or a reproduction of the cited work.

## Primary references

### Project Sid: architecture and measurable social behavior

**Altera.AL (2024), _Project Sid: Many-agent simulations toward AI civilization_, arXiv:2411.00114v1, 31 October 2024.** [Versioned paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.00114v1), [full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2411.00114v1), [official repository](https://github.com/altera-al/project-sid).

Sections 2–3 describe PIANO modules operating at different time scales over shared agent state, a decision controller coordinating outputs, and action awareness comparing expected with observed outcomes. These motivate responsive Minecraft execution, coherent action/message decisions, and physical outcome checks. Hearthbound's proposed bounded modules and versioned records are an adaptation; they do not require adopting the paper's LLM controllers or permit concurrent world mutation outside Forge's execution rules.

Sections 4–5 examine social perception, specialization, collective rules, and information propagation. The measurements need their original qualifications: role labels are inferred from agent-generated social goals (§8.2); some social scores are model judgments; and the large propagation analysis is one 500-agent run (§5.3). Section 7 explicitly limits claims of de novo societal innovation because the foundation models already contain human knowledge. Treat these as hypotheses and measurement examples. Hearthbound must separately demonstrate persistent, behaviorally verified organization and any benefits from communication or learning.

The official repository contains the technical report and assets. It is not a source release of runnable PIANO agents. The paper's abstract and body expand the PIANO acronym differently; this contract uses the acronym without assigning one expansion authority.

### MultiAgentBench: benchmark and evaluation

**Kunlun Zhu, Hongyi Du, Zhaochen Hong, Xiaocheng Yang, Shuyi Guo, Zhe Wang, Zhenhailong Wang, Cheng Qian, Xiangru Tang, Heng Ji, and Jiaxuan You (2025), _MultiAgentBench: Evaluating the Collaboration and Competition of LLM agents_.** ACL 2025, pp. 8580–8622. [Published paper and metadata](https://aclanthology.org/2025.acl-long.421/), DOI [10.18653/v1/2025.acl-long.421](https://doi.org/10.18653/v1/2025.acl-long.421).

The benchmark distinguishes task completion, milestone contributions, and coordination metrics. It compares star, chain, tree, and graph protocols. Its reported graph advantage is scoped to the research scenario; it is not a universal topology ranking. Hearthbound should test topology as an intervention under its own observation, workload, and budget constraints. Dossier P42 explains why the milestone participation denominator must remain distinct from completion.

For a milestone set of size `M`, `N` citizens, and `n_j` milestones credited to citizen `j`, the MARBLE-style average participation is `sum(n_j) / (N * M)`. Therefore, four independently completed milestones each credited to one of four citizens give `4 / 16 = 0.25`, while milestone completion is `4 / 4 = 1`. This is an arithmetic example, not a result from a new run. Multiple validated contributors may raise participation; merely adding participants or messages must not award additional contribution credit.

### MARBLE: maintained implementation

**MARBLE — Multi-Agent CooRdination Backbone with LLM Engine.** [Official repository](https://github.com/ulab-uiuc/MARBLE). This implements the MultiAgentBench research effort and is not a third independent experimental replication.

The November 2026 README documents repairs to routing, scoring, and Minecraft task data, including a file that repeated task 0. Record the exact upstream commit, dataset hash, scenario IDs, scorer version, and configuration before any reproduction. These maintenance statements do not establish when a bug was introduced or that the published ACL results used the affected version. Keep the ACL publication, the March 2025 arXiv v1, the November 2026 arXiv v2, and current code distinct.

H. H. Pham, “Software Agents for Internet-Based Systems and Their Design,” Chapter 5 in Lakhmi C. Jain, Zhengxin Chen, and Nikhil Ichalkaranje (eds.), _Intelligent Agents and Their Applications_ (2002), book DOI [10.1007/978-3-7908-1786-7](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-7908-1786-7). ## Evidence gates for the next phase

1. **Reliable physical accounting.** Repeat the four-citizen scenario in independent fresh worlds on a pinned revision. Distinguish the known physical-completion/task-accounting mismatch from setup errors, timeouts, conservation violations, and other runtime failures. Preserve all outcomes and explicit denominators.
2. **Actual communication records.** Record per-recipient delivery, consumption, and decision use separately from emission. Link use to a task/action and an independent physical witness. Do not award completed-task credit for sending, acknowledging, or repeating a claim.
3. **Controlled collective-performance comparisons.** Compare isolated workers with communication protocols under matched seeds, resources, policies, and budget rules. Measure actual output, completion, time, action counts, message costs, and failures. A message followed by a successful action supplies provenance; a controlled difference across independent worlds supplies evidence about benefit.
4. **Memory and learning experiments.** Freeze policy checkpoints for evaluation; reset state between independent trials; test bounded memory, stale/false information, and no-message controls. Separate training seeds from world seeds and training computation from evaluation costs. Keep LLMs deferred.
5. **Strategic and social experiments.** Add economic mechanisms, policy-profile analysis, and persistent organization measures only with defined utilities, private/public information, and appropriate controls. Programmed roles, a learned controller, and an emergent institution are separate claims. No de novo emergence, human social validity, equilibrium, or causal improvement follows from a single successful colony or from passing contract tests.

This ordering preserves existing Minecraft mechanics while making future claims answerable from observable, reproducible evidence.
