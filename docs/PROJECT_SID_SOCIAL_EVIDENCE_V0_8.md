## Verified source ledger

Reviewed 2026-11-22 (America/Chicago). Primary Project Sid source: Altera.AL, *Project Sid: Many-agent simulations toward AI civilization*, [arXiv:2411.00114v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2411.00114v1), and the [author repository](https://github.com/altera-al/project-sid) containing `2024-10-31.pdf`, README and visual abstract. The repository describes a technical report; it is not a released PIANO controller implementation. Pin the arXiv version for citations; do not import an unrelated repository named SID.

The report's §§2–4 describe concurrent modules, a bottlenecked decision process, memory, action awareness and social awareness. Its action-awareness comparison motivates checking intended versus observed actions. Social ablations motivate testing information access and memory, not assuming they help. §5 studies specialization, collective rules and cultural transmission under specified scenarios. Some role labels and social measures use LLM interpretation; scenario goals and seeded knowledge matter. Those reported findings are neither Hearthbound measurements nor proof of human social validity.

Hearthbound adaptation: preserve bounded recipient-owned observations, distinguish report delivery from decision use, bind decisions to physical jobs, and test communication/memory ablations before promoting a policy. No LLM orchestration, copied PIANO implementation, personality assignment or political institution is introduced here.

Co-primary evaluation reference: Zhu et al., [MultiAgentBench, ACL 2025](https://aclanthology.org/2025.acl-long.421/), with [MARBLE](https://github.com/ulab-uiuc/MARBLE) as its separately versioned implementation. Topology results are scenario-specific, not a universal graph advantage. Hearthbound must compare frozen policies under declared information routes and budgets, with physical completion separate from contribution and cost.

## Implemented and deliberately unmeasured

Explicit source reports already exist in Minecraft. v0.8 makes their delivery provenance exportable and preserves the selected report in the job's persisted pre-assignment receipt. The live two-citizen fixture exports two deliveries, a selected report/job linkage and independently checked endpoint changes. Its frozen simulator-trained source selector remains operator-triggered. Inbox memory stays bounded at 16 reports with the existing expiry/restart boundaries. The archived fixture transcript preserves deliveries beyond that temporary inbox; it is not a global lifetime social journal.

A directed exchange edge is observable; a friendship, organization, learned collaboration, truthful incentive or societal emergence is not established. Organization membership stays null. A later organization analysis must use repeated exchanges plus explicit affiliation/institution events and time windows, with births, exits and uncertain assignments; degree alone cannot establish membership. Such groups must remain optional outcomes, not mandatory success criteria.

Next promotion gates: matched physical no-message and shuffled-message rollouts, memory on/off, alternative routing with identical policies and declared budgets, multiple independent seeds, and retained failed trials. The withheld arm in the present fixture only establishes abstention; it is not an equal-horizon physical counterfactual. Therefore `physical_improvement` stays null even after a successful haul.

Three independent fresh-world four-citizen jobs begin a reliability pilot. Report every planned world and every attempt. Terminal four-item delivery with only three accounted jobs is the specific unresolved failure; transient partial progress is not. Null recurrence does not prove repair. No Minecraft inventory manipulation, forced completion, new rewards, or autonomous policy change is added by this work.
